NIFTY NO BRAINER RATIO — RULEBOOK

At 15:16 IST on the last Friday (previous trading day if that Friday is not
tradable), use the next month's NIFTY monthly expiry exactly as supplied by the
exchange contract master. Select the nearest 100-point ATM; an ATM at a 50
multiple is rounded upward. Buy one CE at ATM+300, sell two CEs at +300 beyond
that, and buy one far CE. The far CE is the 500-multiple in sell+800..sell+1400
closest to sell+1000, ties going farther.

The deployed-capital denominator is the broker's margin for the complete
1:-2:1 structure, with the method recorded. Premium is credit minus debit,
including lot size. A debit or credit above 1% of margin is not eligible: an
excess debit skips the month; an excess credit shifts the near buy by 100 points
and keeps the sell gap at 300, repeating until compliant or MAX_SHIFT_STEPS is
exhausted. Hedge recalculation on shift is configurable and defaults true.

Exit all legs at +2.5% or -3.0% of margin, using the first available fill after
a breach (gap-through can exceed -3%), or at MAX_HOLD_DAYS (run 18 and 19), or
expiry. There are no adjustments, rolls, re-entry, partial exits, or overlap.
The system is paper-only unless explicitly enabled after the R20 adoption gate.

Assumptions: previous-trading-day holiday fallback, calendar hold days, and
15:16 time-of-day for forced exits. Ambiguities: the video's sell 24,700 to
hedge 26,000 example conflicts with the formal 500-grid algorithm; the default
is the formal algorithm. Contract master, broker margin, and period lot size are
mandatory inputs; no weekday or lot size is hard-coded.
